# Design Rules

The active site uses al-folio at the repository root and Quartz under
`quartz/`. Keep the visual language consistent with each system: al-folio pages
and Sass use `_pages/`, `_config.yml`, `assets/css/`, and `_sass/`; Notes use
`quartz/quartz.config.yaml` and Quartz components. Do not create a parallel
theme or an `appearance/` directory.

## Avoid

Excessive gradients, glowing AI-brain / generic-atom stock imagery, animations
without purpose, dozens of colors, huge résumé blocks, excessive badges, skill
progress bars pretending to quantify expertise, corporate jargon.

## Prefer

Modern, scientific, personal, warm, restrained color use, strong typography,
generous whitespace, real photographs and real scientific figures over stock
imagery.

Full rationale and future direction: `docs/DESIGN-GUIDE.md`.
