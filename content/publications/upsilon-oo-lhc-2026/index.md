abstract: |
  We study bottomonium $\Upsilon(nS)$ production in O+O collisions at
  $\sqrt{s_{NN}}=5.36$ TeV, including cold nuclear-matter effects and
  final-state interactions in a short-lived quark-gluon plasma. The study
  compares QTRAJ-NLO open-quantum-system calculations and TAMU-NP semiclassical
  rates in a shared hydrodynamic background.
