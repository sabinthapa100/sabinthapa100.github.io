## Learning path

- Color, quarks, and gluons
- SU(3) gauge symmetry and the non-Abelian field strength
- Gluon self-interactions
- Renormalization and the running coupling
- Asymptotic freedom and the long-distance regime
- Chiral symmetry, hadronization, and confinement
- Perturbative methods, lattice QCD, and effective theories
- QCD at finite temperature and density
