## Learning path

- QCD phase structure and finite-temperature QCD
- Deconfinement, chiral symmetry, and the QCD phase diagram
- Relativistic nuclear collisions and initial conditions
- Hydrodynamics and collective observables
- Hard probes, jets, and electromagnetic probes
- Heavy flavor and quarkonium
- System-size scans and open questions in small systems

## Research bridge

Keep claims about QGP formation, hydrodynamization, or small-system collectivity tied to specific reviews and experimental papers. [[reference-shelf#cern-heavy-ions|CERN]] and [[reference-shelf#bnl-rhic|Brookhaven]] provide institutional context, not substitutes for technical evidence.

Avoid shorthand such as “RHIC proved QGP.” When describing historical evidence, use the wording and scope supported by the cited experiment papers or review.
