## Long-term topics

- Natural units, relativistic kinematics, and phase space
- Cross sections, decay widths, and resonance line shapes
- Feynman rules, Green functions, and spectral functions
- Monte Carlo methods and uncertainty propagation
- Kinetic theory, Boltzmann equations, and hydrodynamics
- Lattice methods and effective field theories
- Statistical inference and reproducible computation
