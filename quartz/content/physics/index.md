These are learning notes beyond my immediate research, built from named textbooks, lecture notes, and papers rather than generated summaries.

## Long-term paths

- **Analytical mechanics and geometry of motion:** variational principles, constraints, canonical structure, and symmetries.
- **Classical electrodynamics and field geometry:** Maxwell's equations, potentials, radiation, and covariant formulations.
- **Statistical mechanics, information, and non-equilibrium:** ensembles, fluctuations, transport, and irreversible processes.
- **Spacetime and general relativity:** special relativity, geometry, curvature, and gravitational dynamics.
- **Condensed matter and collective phenomena:** phases, symmetry breaking, quasiparticles, and emergent behavior.

These are roadmaps, not claims that the material is already developed.
