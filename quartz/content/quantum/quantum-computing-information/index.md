## Learning path

1. Quantum states, measurement, and entanglement
2. Quantum circuits and information-theoretic concepts
3. Canonical algorithms and Hamiltonian simulation
4. Channels, noise, and error correction
5. Variational methods and quantum simulation
6. Quantum and high-performance computing

Use [[reference-shelf#preskill-quantum|Preskill]] and [[reference-shelf#nielsen-chuang|Nielsen and Chuang]] for theory, then consult [[reference-shelf#ibm-quantum-learning|IBM Quantum Learning]] for current course material and implementation practice.
