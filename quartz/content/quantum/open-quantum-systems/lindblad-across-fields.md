## Question

Which mathematical structures in Markovian open-system dynamics are reused across fields, and which similarities are only analogies?

## Foundation to study first

Use the [[reference-shelf#oqs-jupyterbook|OQS JupyterBook]] as one pedagogical source, then add canonical sources for each application before making comparisons.

## Literature-search queue

These are questions to investigate, not claims that the framework is used in each area:

- Quantum optics and atomic physics: identify a primary or review source and the exact open-system model.
- Noisy quantum devices: identify when a Lindblad description is appropriate and where it fails.
- Heavy-quarkonium transport: trace the specific effective theory and transport papers before describing the connection.
- Neutrinos, neutron stars, and cosmology: leave as open search questions until serious sources are found.

## Comparison to make after sourcing

For each sourced example, compare the system/environment split, approximations, dynamical regime, and observable. Do not infer a shared physical mechanism solely from a shared equation form.
