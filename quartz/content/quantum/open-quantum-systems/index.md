## Learning path

1. Closed-system states and density operators
2. System plus environment and reduced states
3. Partial trace and dynamical maps
4. Markov approximations and master equations
5. Conditions for GKSL/Lindblad evolution
6. Quantum trajectories and computational examples

The [[reference-shelf#oqs-jupyterbook|OQS JupyterBook]] is a substantial pedagogical source with theory and computational projects. Pair it with canonical references; do not treat one course as the final authority on every approximation.

## Research bridge

A later, source-verified path may connect reduced dynamics to quarkonium transport and QTraj. Add that connection only after identifying the specific papers and derivations that support it.
