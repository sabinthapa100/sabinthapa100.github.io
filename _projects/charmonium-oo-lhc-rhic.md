An ongoing study of inclusive J/ψ (charmonium) production and modification
in O+O collisions at the LHC (√s_NN = 5.36 TeV) and RHIC (√s_NN = 200 GeV),
combining cold-nuclear-matter effects with hot-medium suppression and
regeneration, including the nonprompt (b-hadron feed-down) contribution,
using nonperturbative transport on a 3+1D aHydroQP hydrodynamic background.

**Status**: manuscript in preparation, not yet submitted.

**Collaborators**: Sabin Thapa, Biaogang Wu, Kaiyu Fu, Ramona Vogt, Ralf Rapp.
