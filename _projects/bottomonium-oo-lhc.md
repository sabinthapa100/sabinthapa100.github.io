An ongoing study of Υ(nS) (bottomonium) production and modification in O+O
collisions at √s_NN = 5.36 TeV, combining cold-nuclear-matter effects with
hot-medium transport — the QTRAJ-NLO open-quantum-system solver and
TAMU-NP semiclassical transport, on a 3+1D aHydroQP hydrodynamic
background, including regeneration.

**Status**: manuscript in preparation, not yet submitted.

**Collaborators**: Sabin Thapa, Biaogang Wu, Ramona Vogt, Ralf Rapp.

See [Research](/research/) for the broader physics questions this connects to.
