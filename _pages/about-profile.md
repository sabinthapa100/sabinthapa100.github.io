I am a PhD candidate in Physics at Kent State University, where I study quarkonium production and transport in high-energy nuclear collisions. I work on bottomonium and charmonium across small, light-ion, and heavy-ion systems, using open-quantum-system and semiclassical transport with hydrodynamic backgrounds.

## Education

- **PhD in Physics**, Kent State University, August 15, 2019–expected December 30, 2026. Current advisors: Dr. Andrew Hanlon and Dr. Ramona Vogt. I previously worked with Prof. Michael Strickland through Spring 2024.
- **M.A. in Physics**, Kent State University, August 15, 2019–May 18, 2021.
- **B.Sc. in Physics (distinction)**, Amrit Campus, Tribhuvan University, 2014–2018. Minors in mathematics and chemistry.

## Research & Current Interests

My established research centers on nuclear and high-energy physics, QCD matter, heavy-ion collisions, heavy flavor, quarkonium, and quantum transport. I am also exploring AI for science and physics, AI safety, quantum computing, and radiation physics as broader interests and learning areas.
